We collect information that you voluntarily provide when inquiring about or booking photography sessions, including:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, phone number, and preferred contact times.
  • Session & Event Details: Desired shoot dates, venue locations, property addresses for real estate listings, timeline notes, and creative preferences.
  • Photographic Media: Digital captures, raw photographs, and finished high-resolution edits created during your booked sessions.
  • Billing & Invoicing Details: Invoicing information and transaction records processed securely via reputable third-party processors. We do not store sensitive payment card numbers on our servers.

We use your information for legitimate photography business operations, including:

  • Displaying authentic sample work in our portfolio and marketing materials (subject to client privacy agreements).

We honor client discretion. We strictly respect all agreed privacy preferences.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with trusted service providers strictly necessary to perform operations, such as secure cloud-hosted client galleries, professional print fulfillment labs, and encrypted payment processors.

5. Data Security

We maintain industry-standard physical and technical safeguards to secure your personal information and digital image archives from unauthorized access or accidental loss. Client galleries are secured with unique direct links and optional PIN protection.
